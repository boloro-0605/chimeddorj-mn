A Portrait of Our Culture and Technology

Chimeddorj Munkhjargal · At a British Embassy event · Full transcript · Updated 2026

Key Ideas

Culture as a Factor in Development

Using Hofstede's comparative research methodology, a 2014 study breaks down the cultural profile of Mongolians.

Low Power Distance

Mongolians don't revere authority — in this respect similar to the British — which supports open communication within organizations and has a positive effect on development.

Among the World's Highest in Masculinity

A highly competitive disposition, but possibly weaker in care and consideration for others — with a negative effect on social cohesion.

High Individualism

Positive for development based on individual initiative, but with hidden effects on attitudes toward collective decisions and rules.

Risk of "Toxic Complexity"

Weak emotional recognition, combined with high individualism and masculinity, may negatively affect social cohesion.

Technology Is Pushing Culture Toward "Today"

Mongolians spend an average of 4 hours a day on social media, which is intensifying cultural traits and weakening long-term thinking.
