Interview · 2013 · Labor Research Institute

The Labor Market Barometer: In-Demand Skills and Workforce Shortages

Chimeddorj Munkhjargal · An interview on the labor market · Full transcript · Updated 2026

Key Ideas

What the barometer survey is

The annual labor market demand assessment — the barometer survey — was that year organized and carried out by the Labor Research Institute. Drawing on a questionnaire put to organizations planning to take on new staff, it calculated demand for the twelve months from June 2013 to June 2014.

The sectors with the highest demand

Demand for labor was highest in construction and infrastructure, followed by processing and manufacturing. For that year, the sector with the fastest growth was calculated to be agriculture.

The numbers in construction

Construction that year showed a total requirement of roughly 197,000 workers, while domestic construction and infrastructure companies employed more than 160,000 — leaving a visible shortfall of some thirty thousand workers. The survey also showed those companies planning to take on more than 20,000 new staff.

Domestic labor supply falls short

Looking at construction alone makes it plain that the domestic supply of labor is inadequate. Judging by the structural growth of Mongolia's economy at the time, the need for skilled workers stood at an equally high level in other sectors.

The occupations in least demand

Among the most sought-after occupations were personnel working in light industry and the roads sector, while lawyers, journalists, and artists were named among the occupations in least demand.

The gap facing social science graduates

For university graduates, and particularly those finishing in the social sciences, the supply of jobs was not adequate — the scarcity of opportunities to work in their own field points to a gap between what higher education produces and what the labor market demands.
