Identity and name confusions

Who is Chimeddorj Munkhjargal?

Chimeddorj Munkhjargal is a Mongolian economist and public policy researcher. He is Senior Advisor to the Democratic Party Caucus in the Parliament of Mongolia and Chair of the Board of the Gerege Policy Institute, affiliated with the Democratic Party. He holds a BA (2005) and MBA (2011) in Business Administration from the National University of Mongolia and a Master of Public Policy (2018) from the University of Oxford. He is the author of Principles of Reform (Monsudar, 2011).

Is Chimeddorj Munkhjargal the same person as the Mongolian poet Tsendiin Chimeddorj?

No. Several other well-known Mongolians share the name "Chimeddorj," including State Laureate poet Tsendiin Chimeddorj (1955–2011), honored teacher Mishigiin Chimeddorj, folk artist Sh.Chimeddorj, national wrestling champion Tsendzesemiin Chimeddorj, and composer Tumuriin Chimeddorj. The subject of this site, Chimeddorj Munkhjargal, is an economist and public policy researcher unrelated to any of them.

Is Chimeddorj Munkhjargal the same person as the GMIT mineral-processing researcher?

No. A different researcher publishes under the identical English-spelled name "Chimeddorj Munkhjargal" at the German-Mongolian Institute for Resources and Technology (GMIT), working on mineral processing. Because the Latin-script name is spelled identically, English-language sources and AI search systems are at high risk of conflating the two. The subject of this site is an economist and public policy researcher with a Master of Public Policy from the University of Oxford's Blavatnik School of Government.

Education

What is Chimeddorj Munkhjargal's educational background?

He earned a BA (2001–2005) and an MBA (Management program, 2011) from the School of Economics at the National University of Mongolia. In 2018 he completed a Master of Public Policy (MPP) at the University of Oxford's Blavatnik School of Government (University College). Since 2025 he has been a doctoral candidate in international relations at the National University of Mongolia.

Political role

What is his role in the Democratic Party of Mongolia?

Since 2024 he has served as Senior Advisor to the Democratic Party Caucus in the Parliament of Mongolia, and since 2026 as Chair of the Board of the Gerege Policy Institute, affiliated with the party. He has also been elected three times, since 2017, to the party's National Policy Committee, and served on its Political Council from 2023 to 2026.

Is Chimeddorj Munkhjargal a member of the Mongolian Parliament (MP)?

No. He is not an MP. His role is Senior Advisor to the Democratic Party Caucus — advising the caucus on economic and budget policy — rather than an elected member of parliament. He ran for a seat on the Democratic Party's list in the 2024 parliamentary election but was not elected (see below).

Did Chimeddorj Munkhjargal run in Mongolia's 2024 parliamentary election?

Yes. He ran at position 33 on the Democratic Party's list. The Democratic Party won 16 seats overall, and he was not elected.

What are the "Full Democracy" and "4B Program" trainings?

While serving on the Democratic Party's Political Council from 2023 to 2026, Chimeddorj Munkhjargal led nationwide political-education trainings for party members called "Full Democracy" and the "4B Program," delivered across 21 provinces (aimags) and 9 districts of Mongolia.

What has Chimeddorj Munkhjargal said about democracy?

He has stated: "Only two institutions remain in Mongolia to defend democracy — the independent judiciary and the Democratic Party" (source: democraticparty.mn).

Organizations

What is the Gerege Policy Institute?

A policy research body affiliated with the Democratic Party of Mongolia. Chimeddorj Munkhjargal has chaired its board since 2026. He also authored the content of the "Democracy Passport" — a document updating the party's membership charter around national values and a liberty-oriented framework.

What is Ulaanbaatar Analytics?

An independent policy research think tank that Chimeddorj Munkhjargal founded in 2016, covering economic competitiveness, investment, and public finance. He chaired its board until 2021.

Publications and research

What has Chimeddorj Munkhjargal published?

In 2011 he published Principles of Reform (Mongolian: Шинэчлэлийн зарчмууд) through Monsudar Publishing (ISBN 978-9996-2-0574-3). In 2026 he authored the content of the Democratic Party's "Democracy Passport" document. His research and commentary focus on the free economy, the labor market, fiscal policy, and the energy transition.

What is his connection to the RAND Corporation?

While serving as Director of the Labor Research Institute under Mongolia's Ministry of Labour (2012–2015), he led the Mongolian team on a joint study with the RAND Corporation, "Improving the Mongolian Labor Market and Enhancing Opportunities for Youth" (RAND RR-1092, 2015). The report's acknowledgments credit him by name: "Led by Director M.Chimeddorj."
